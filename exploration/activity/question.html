Agent question mark

An idle agent is in one of three states, and today all three show the same amber envelope: it has a question, it needs your approval (a tool approval or a plan), or its turn ended. After five rounds each has its own mark. The speech bubble takes the question, a crested shield takes the approval, and the envelope keeps the finished turn it already shows.

All three are amber and static, and the outline alone tells them apart at 12px. The shield follows lucide's shield proportions (crested top, straight sides, pointed base), redrawn in this set's own arcs. It is 18 wide like every indicator and 20 tall, overshooting the slot by a unit at the peak and the point, as a typeface does for a pointed letter.

Those three are for surfaces that show one agent. The surfaces that count agents, the desktop sidebar and the Agent Monitor, have room for one idle count, and they keep drawing it with the envelope, covering all three kinds, as they do today. Mail means "there is something for you", which is what that count says. A bell, an inbox tray, the closed ring and an amber dot were drawn for that job and set aside.
